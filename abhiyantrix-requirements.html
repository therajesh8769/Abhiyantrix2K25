Abhiyantrix 2027 — Website Update

Content & asset requirements checklist for the website redesign

Prepared: 24 November 2026

1 Event Information
  • Event Date & Venue Required
    Full dates (day, month, year) and venue/campus name for Abhiyantrix 2027.
  • Theme / Tagline Required
    New theme or tagline for 2027. If keeping the space/tech theme, confirm it.
  • About / Description Required
    Updated "About Abhiyantrix" paragraph — what the fest is, who organises it, which college.
2 Events & Schedule
  • Event List Required
    For each event: name, category (Technical / Non-Technical / Workshop / Cultural), short description (2–3 lines), rules or format, and team size.
  • Event Coordinators Required
    Coordinator name(s) and phone number for each event.
  • Event Schedule / Timeline Optional
    Day-wise schedule with time slots, if available.
  • Prize Details Optional
    Prize pool or prize breakdown per event, if decided.
3 Registration
  • Registration Link / Method Required
    Google Form URL, external platform link, or on-site registration details.
  • Registration Fee Required
    Fee per person or per team, and whether it covers all events or is per-event.
  • Registration Deadline Optional
    Last date to register, early-bird cutoff if any.
4 Branding & Design
  • Logo Required
    Updated Abhiyantrix 2027 logo file (PNG with transparent background preferred).
  • Color Scheme / Visual Direction Required
    Keep the current space/blue theme or switch to something new? Share any mood boards or references.
  • Updated Brochure PDF Optional
    New downloadable brochure to replace the 2026 version.
  • Photos / Media Optional
    Event photos from previous years, campus photos, or promo images.
5 Contact & Social
  • Contact Details Required
    Main organiser name(s), phone numbers, and email address for enquiries.
  • Social Media Links Required
    Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Twitter/X, or any other active handles.
6 Additional Sections
  • Sponsors / Partners Optional
    Sponsor names and logos, with tier levels (Title / Gold / Silver) if applicable.
  • Guest Speakers / Chief Guest Optional
    Names, designations, and photos of any invited guests or speakers.
  • Core Team / Organisers Optional
    Team member names, roles, and photos for an "Our Team" section.
  • Past Highlights / Gallery Optional
    Highlight reel, stats (footfall, participants), or photo gallery from previous editions.
  • FAQ Optional
    Common questions: accommodation, food, transport, eligibility, refund policy.
